WHY WE BUILT SUKOON · क्यों बनाया

India has 1.4 billion people. And one psychiatrist for every 100,000 of them. भारत में 1.4 अरब लोग हैं। और हर 1 लाख पर सिर्फ़ एक मनोचिकित्सक।

Mental health in India isn't a feature gap. It's a public-health crisis. Sukoon is not therapy. It is not a doctor. But it is a free, private, bilingual sanctuary that anyone with a phone can open at 2am — without a signup, without a credit card, without telling anyone. सुकून थेरपी नहीं है। डॉक्टर नहीं है। लेकिन यह एक मुफ़्त, निजी, द्विभाषी आश्रय है — रात 2 बजे एक टैप दूर।

Questions सवाल

What is Sukoon · सुकून क्या है

Sukoon is a free, bilingual sanctuary for difficult moments. Quiet meditations for anxiety, panic, grief and ordinary heaviness. Sleep stories from Indian places and the natural world. Breath practices with on-screen guidance. Yoga sequences and Patanjali sutras. A patient AI friend who listens when no human is awake. All free. All forever.

Is it really free · क्या यह सच में मुफ़्त है

Yes. Forever. There is no paid tier. No premium content locked. No "pro" upgrade. Sukoon is funded as a public-interest project, not as a business. Free, the way a public park is free.

Does Sukoon store my data · क्या सुकून मेरा डेटा रखता है

No account. No login. The only thing stored is your streak count, and only on your device. Sukoon's servers do not know who you are. Read the full privacy posture.

Is this therapy · क्या यह थेरपी है

No. Sukoon is a wellness sanctuary, not a clinical service. If you need therapy, please see a registered mental health professional. The crisis helplines above — iCall, Vandrevala Foundation, AASRA — can also help you find one.

Who built Sukoon · सुकून किसने बनाया

A small team in India. We do not put our faces on this site because Sukoon is not about us. If you want to know more, write to hello@sukoon.cloud.
